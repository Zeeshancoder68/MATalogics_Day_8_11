# CloudFlow Customer Support Knowledge Base

## 1. Company Overview

CloudFlow is a fictional software company used for this AI customer support automation project.

CloudFlow provides software tools designed to help businesses manage their work and customer operations through a centralized platform.

The AI customer support bot should only provide information contained in this knowledge base. If the required information is not available, the bot must not guess or invent an answer.

---

# 2. Pricing Plans

## Starter Plan

Price: $19/month

The Starter plan is designed for customers who need the core CloudFlow experience for basic business use.

## Professional Plan

Price: $49/month

The Professional plan is designed for customers who need additional capabilities for growing business operations.

## Business Plan

Price: $149/month

The Business plan is designed for larger business requirements and more advanced operational use.

### Important Pricing Rule

The chatbot must only provide the pricing information listed above.

Do not invent discounts, annual pricing, additional plans, promotional pricing, taxes, refunds, or other pricing details unless they are explicitly provided in this knowledge base.

If a customer asks for pricing information that is not available here, explain that the available information is limited and offer to create a support request.

Instead, say:

"I don't have enough information to answer that accurately. I can create a support request for you."

# 7. Common Troubleshooting Solutions

## Login Problem

If a customer is having trouble logging in:

1. Confirm that the customer is using the correct account credentials.
2. Ask the customer to try logging in again.
3. If the problem continues, offer to create a support request.

## Account Access Problem

If a customer cannot access their account:

1. Confirm that they are using the correct account credentials.
2. Ask them to try again.
3. If they still cannot access the account, create or offer to create a support request.

Account access problems should be treated as high-priority support issues when a support ticket is created.

## General Software Problem

If the customer reports a general software problem:

1. Ask the customer to retry the affected action.
2. Ask the customer to describe what they were trying to do.
3. If the problem continues or cannot be resolved using the available information, offer to create a support request.

## System Completely Down

If the customer reports that the entire CloudFlow system is completely down, the issue should be treated as a Critical priority support request.

The chatbot should collect the customer's name, email, and problem and send the request for support escalation.

## Feature Not Working

If a known CloudFlow feature is not working:

1. Ask the customer to retry the action.
2. Ask them to describe the problem.
3. If the problem continues, offer to create a support request.

If the customer reports that an unknown or undocumented feature is missing, the chatbot must not claim that the feature exists.

---

# 8. Support Request Rule

When the chatbot cannot solve a customer's problem using the available knowledge, it should say:

"I don't have enough information to answer that accurately. I can create a support request for you."

If the customer agrees, collect the information required for the support request.

For human escalation, collect:

* Name
* Email
* Problem

---

# 9. Human Escalation

If the customer says that they want to talk to a human, the chatbot must collect:

* Name
* Email
* Problem

The chatbot should then submit the support request to the Zapier automation.

The request will be stored in the Support Tickets table.

---

# 10. Support Priority Rules

When a support request is created, determine its priority using the following rules.

## Critical

If the customer reports:

"System completely down"

Priority = Critical

## High

If the customer reports:

"Can't access account"

Priority = High

## Medium

Normal question or normal support issue.

Priority = Medium

## Low

General request.

Priority = Low

When the customer's issue does not clearly match a Critical or High condition, do not exaggerate its severity. Use Medium for a normal support issue or Low for a general request.
